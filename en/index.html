Health insurance · for broker clients

Health insurance app: an insurance consultant in your pocket

A mobile app for employees of companies insured by Companion. People find out for themselves what their insurance covers, find a clinic and get help if they are refused.

Status: under App Store reviewPlatforms: iOS, AndroidLanguage: Ukrainian
What was optimized

Questions that used to go to a manager

Before

  • The client's employee calls the broker's manager: “is this test covered”, “which clinic can I go to”.
  • The policy and the program sit as a PDF somewhere in HR's mailbox.
  • After a payment refusal, the person cannot tell whether the insurer is right.

After

  • An answer in the chat within seconds, based on the documents of their own program.
  • Policy, coverage, limits and clinics are always on the phone.
  • When a claim is refused, the AI checks the refusal against the program terms and passes the disputed case to the broker.
How the AI chat works

Answers only from your program's documents

A person writes a question in ordinary words, for example naming a diagnosis or a test. The system finds the relevant passages in that employee's own contract and insurance program and answers: covered or not, with what limit, at which clinics, and what to do next.

If the documents have no answer, the AI says so and suggests writing to the broker. It does not diagnose or advise treatment: it is a guide to the insurance, not a doctor.

A separate scenario for refusals. If a service was not paid for, the AI checks what the program says, and if the refusal contradicts the terms, it says so directly and passes the case to the broker's team.

AI chat · example
Is a knee MRI covered? The doctor gave me a referral.
Yes, under your program an MRI is covered when medically indicated.

What to do: call Assistance to approve the scan and get a referral to a clinic from your program.
And if Assistance refuses?
Send me the reason for the refusal and I will check it against the program terms. If you disagree with the decision, the broker's team will review it.

Health insurance · tenders

Health insurance tender: all offers in one table

An internal broker tool. A client company chooses insurance for its employees, 10–15 insurers send offers, and the system merges them for a fair comparison.

Status: working, the first full run on a real tender is under wayUsers: Companion's brokers
What was optimized

Manually comparing dozens of Excel files

Before

  • Each insurer sends its own Excel offer with its own structure and wording.
  • An expert copies the terms into a shared table by hand and looks for what is covered where.
  • It is easy to miss a limit or an exclusion hidden in a note.

After

  • Files are uploaded as they are, with no fitting to a template.
  • Terms are sorted into a single list: covered, covered with a limit, not covered, unclear.
  • Every value links to a sheet and a cell of the source file and can be checked in one click.
How it works

The broker's path, step by step

  1. Client request

    We upload the client's requirements and build a matrix of terms.

  2. Choosing insurers

    We mark the participants from the directory of insurance companies.

  3. Uploading offers

    We drag each insurer's file onto it.

  4. Comparison

    The AI matches the insurers' wording against the client's terms.

  5. Expert decision

    Where the system is unsure, the broker answers right in the table.

  6. Clinics and service

    Clinic coverage by city and a 45-question assistance questionnaire.

Main ruleThe AI can extract and sort data, but only a person marks it as verified. An empty cell does not turn into zero, and an uncertain match is flagged as uncertain.
For the client

The client gets a clear recommendation

In the end the broker shows who has better coverage, exclusions, clinics, service and price, and gives a recommendation with arguments. The decision stays with the client.

The insurer's service questionnaire is compared as it is: if one company writes “83%” and another writes “we don't count” (in Ukrainian), that is visible without any “smoothing over”.

Health insurance · legal review

Contract review: AI finds where the insurer departed from the terms

After the tender the insurer sends a contract. The broker has to make sure it contains all the edits the broker usually insists on. Now the system does this.

Status: working inside the tender toolFormats: Word, Excel
What was optimized

Before

  • A lawyer or a broker reads the whole contract, relying on memory and past emails.
  • The contract arrives in parts, in several files.
  • The letter to the insurer with the edits is written by hand.

After

  • The system compares the contract with the broker's registry of standard edits.
  • Several files are checked as one contract.
  • Ready-made paragraph wording and a letter to the insurer with the edits.
How it works
  1. Upload

    Up to 8 contract files, in the order of the parts.

  2. Finding departures

    Each finding points to its paragraph and an example from past contracts.

  3. New wording

    The AI rewrites the paragraph the way the broker usually demands.

  4. Letter and file

    A letter to the insurer and a Word file with the edits in track-changes mode.

  5. Learning

    Accepted edits are saved and taken into account in later reviews.

Health insurance · back office

Broker accounting: from 1C to the browser

A web system for tracking contracts, payments, insurer reports and managers' commission. It reproduces the workflow of 1C “SB Companion” but works from anywhere and does a lot on its own.

Status: in use by the teamRoles: head, curator, manager
What was optimized

Before

  • 1C opened only through a remote desktop.
  • One person calculated the commission by manager.
  • Insurer reports and payments were reconciled by hand.

After

  • Work happens in the browser; everyone has their own login and permissions.
  • Commission is split between the curator, the manager and the bureau automatically, and the manager sees their own income.
  • The system reconciles reports and bank receipts with contracts.
How it works
  1. Contract

    Policyholder, insurer, amounts, rate, commission and participants' shares, checked to total 100%.

  2. Payment schedule

    The system reminds about overdue payments and contracts due for renewal.

  3. Insurer report

    You upload the report as PDF, Excel or CSV and the system finds the contracts by number.

  4. Bank

    You paste the bank statement and the system finds the receipts and matches them to the right report.

  5. Profit calculation

    Commission for the period is distributed among managers with a breakdown by contract.

How it was moved from 1CThe configuration was available only on screen, with no export. The logic was reconstructed from the forms and checked on a real contract: premium UAH 440.80, commission 110.20, curator 73.28, manager 31.41. It matched 1C to the kopeck.

An AI manager for two villas on Airbnb

One agent looks after guests, the cleaner and the pool technician. It runs in the cloud around the clock and writes to the owner only when a decision is needed.

Status: runs on its own, 2 villasLanguages: Russian, English, Thai

Example conversation
What was optimized

Before

  • The owner answered guests on Airbnb personally.
  • Arranged every cleaning with the cleaner personally and checked it was done.
  • Scheduled the pool technician personally so that he would not disturb the guests.

After

  • The agent drafts replies to guests; the owner just taps “OK”.
  • Cleanings are arranged, reminded and checked without the owner.
  • The owner learns at once, at any time, about a missed cleaning, a breakdown or a refusal.
How it works
  1. Booking

    The agent sees the calendar and guest messages for both villas.

  2. Guests

    Instructions the day before arrival, a selection of places on the second day, answers to questions. Sent after the owner's OK.

  3. Cleaning

    A couple of days ahead it proposes a time to the cleaner, reminds the day before, and on the day waits for “done”.

  4. Pool

    Once a week, at a convenient time and with the guests as the priority. It forwards the pool technician's photo to the owner.

  5. Owner

    Reports and anything that needs a decision go to Telegram.

Safeguards in code, not in the promptQuiet hours, the daily message limit and the ban on repeats are checked by the program before sending. A model can change its mind; a check in code cannot. The agent has been tested on refusals, reschedulings, counter-questions and false reports of “already cleaned”.
